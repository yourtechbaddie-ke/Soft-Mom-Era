# Production Content Rules

Every health article should have:

- Topic
- Audience/age/stage
- Plain-language summary
- Practical steps
- Red flags / when to seek care
- Sources
- Last reviewed date
- Medical reviewer, where applicable
- Region/country applicability
- Version number

## Avoid

- Diagnosis
- False certainty
- Unsourced medical claims
- Replacing emergency care
- Replacing professional medical care
- Personalized medication dosing without a clinically safe workflow
