# Recommended Source Registry

Soft Mom Era should prioritize official and authoritative sources for health and parenting content.

| Source | Primary Use |
|---|---|
| WHO | Maternal, newborn, child health and public health |
| ACOG | Pregnancy, postpartum, reproductive and gynecologic health |
| AAP / HealthyChildren.org | Pediatrics, development, parenting and safety |
| CDC | Vaccines, development, public health and prevention |
| NIH / MedlinePlus | General medical education |
| LactMed | Medication and breastfeeding evidence |
| Local health authority | Country-specific care pathways and emergency guidance |

---

# Production Requirements

For each source:

- Store the canonical article URL.
- Store the source organization.
- Store publication date.
- Store last review date.
- Store source version where applicable.
- Store country/region applicability.
- Record which app topics the source supports.

---

# Medical Content Review

Health content should be reviewed by appropriately qualified professionals before being presented as medical guidance.

Content should also be periodically reviewed because medical recommendations can change.

---

# Region Awareness

Some recommendations vary by country.

The production system should therefore support:

- Country
- Region
- Healthcare system
- Emergency guidance
- Local health authority
- Local terminology

Do not assume that one country's healthcare pathway applies everywhere.

---

# Source Transparency

Aura responses should make it easy for the mother to see where information came from.

Example:

Source:
American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists

Article:
[Her New Glow]

Reviewed:
August 2026

This allows users to verify important information themselves.
